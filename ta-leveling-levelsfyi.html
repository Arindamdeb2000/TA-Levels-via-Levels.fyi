Talent Acquisition Leveling, defined via Levels.fyi

The recruiting career ladder at Google, Microsoft, Salesforce, Amazon, and Meta — from an entry coordinator up through leadership to VP of Talent Acquisition — using the level codes Levels.fyi reports for the Recruiter role at each company.

Level data: Levels.fyi (levels.fyi), company Recruiter pages. As of September 2026.

How Levels.fyi defines these levels

Levels.fyi maps levels across companies by scope and responsibility, and by real employee transfers — not by compensation. The question it answers is: "what level would I transfer to if I switched companies?"

It tracks each company in that company's own codes: Google's L-scale, Microsoft's numeric bands, Meta's IC-scale, Amazon's L-scale. Salesforce publishes no numeric codes on postings (its MTS ladder is engineering-only), so Levels.fyi normalizes Salesforce recruiters onto an L4–L7 scale.

Levels.fyi's Recruiter page covers the individual-contributor levels. The leadership tiers above first-line management — Recruiting Manager up to VP of Talent Acquisition — follow each company's broader level scale; the first management level overlaps the senior IC levels in code.

Amazon's Recruiter entry level is L4 but Levels.fyi reports ≈4–5 years of experience there and ≈8–9 at L5 — Amazon's numbers run about a level "ahead" of Google/Meta for the same scope. Microsoft's base Recruiter track tops at 63 on Levels.fyi; its Technical and Leadership Recruiter tracks extend to ≈67.
